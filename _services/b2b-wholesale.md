Most stores end up treating wholesale as an afterthought, bolted onto a retail theme that was never built for it. That usually means one of two outcomes: the wholesale flow is clumsy and hard for bulk buyers to use, or the retail experience gets cluttered with pricing tiers and options that have nothing to do with a normal customer's visit. Neither is necessary. This is a solved problem: the [WholeNights case study]({{ '/case-studies/wholenights/' | relative_url }}) is a real example of a single Shopify store serving both retail and B2B buyers, tiered pricing, margin visibility, and a dedicated wholesale order flow, without the retail side ever feeling like it was built around someone else's checkout.
