Getting more traffic to a store that isn't converting is expensive. The better path is to understand why the traffic you already have isn't buying — and in most cases, the answers are measurable. Drop-off points in the funnel, friction in the checkout flow, product pages that don't build enough confidence to complete a purchase: these are patterns that show up in data and in UX, and they're fixable. CRO isn't about guessing which button color converts better. It's about diagnosing the actual reasons a visitor doesn't buy, then removing those barriers one by one.
