A platform migration carries real risk if it's handled carelessly: lost SEO rankings from broken URLs and missing redirects, lost customer data from an incomplete transfer, or downtime during a sales window the business can't afford to lose. None of that is inherent to switching platforms — it's what happens when a migration is rushed or done without a proper redirect and data-mapping plan. A properly managed migration moves everything that matters, product data, customers, order history, and search equity, without the store ever going dark.
