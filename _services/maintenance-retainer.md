A Shopify store isn't a project that ends at launch. Themes change, apps update, Shopify itself ships new features that interact with existing customizations in unexpected ways. Stores that go unmonitored quietly accumulate small problems — a broken collection filter, a checkout edge case, a mobile layout that regressed after an update — and those small problems compound. A retainer means there's always someone who knows your store, watching it, handling the ongoing work, so you're not starting from scratch explaining context every time something needs attention.
