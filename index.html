Got Questions?

01/

What’s your usual project timeline?

Most theme development or customization projects are completed within 1 to 4 weeks, depending on the scope and level of detail required. Once I understand your requirements, I’ll share a clear timeline with milestones for design setup, development, testing, and final delivery.

02/

Do you provide ongoing support after the project?

Yes! I offer ongoing maintenance and support for Shopify stores. This can include performance improvements, bug fixes, layout tweaks, and updates to keep your theme running smoothly as your business evolves.

03/

Can you follow my existing brand guidelines?

Definitely. I make sure your Shopify store fully aligns with your brand’s look and feel—colors, typography, layout, and visual tone. Whether you already have detailed brand guidelines or just a reference design, I can implement them precisely and maintain consistency across all pages.

04/

How do you handle revisions and feedback?

Revisions are part of the process. I provide structured feedback rounds where you can review progress and suggest adjustments. I value clear communication and make sure your feedback is implemented quickly and accurately until everything looks and functions as intended.
